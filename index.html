Got questions?

We've got answers.

Measure around the widest part of your dog's neck with a soft tape measure, then add 2 inches for a comfortable fit. Our size guide: XS (8–10"), S (10–13"), M (13–17"), L (17–21"), XL (21–26"). If your dog is between sizes, we recommend sizing up — you can always adjust the buckle. Every order comes with a detailed sizing card.
Vegetable-tanned leather is naturally water-resistant, not waterproof. Your dog can splash in puddles and run through light rain with no issues. For swimming or regular water exposure, we recommend removing the collar and drying it naturally away from direct heat. We include a small tin of our beeswax conditioner with every order — using it once a month keeps the leather supple and maximally water-resistant.
We ship all in-stock orders within 24 hours on business days. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express (1–2 day) and next-day options are available at checkout. Engraved orders take one additional business day for engraving. International orders typically arrive in 7–14 business days depending on your country.
We offer a 60-day return window on all products — even personalized ones. If the fit isn't right, the color isn't what you hoped, or your dog just doesn't love it (rare, but it happens), send it back for a full refund or free exchange. We pay for return shipping. No questions asked. We believe if you're not 100% happy, we haven't done our job.
Our guarantee covers manufacturing defects, hardware failure, and stitching issues for the lifetime of the product. If the buckle breaks, a seam splits, or the D-ring fails — we replace it free, no matter when you bought it. Normal wear and patina development are features, not defects. The only thing not covered is intentional damage or use as a chew toy (we make those too — they're excellent).
All PAWVARA orders arrive in our signature gift packaging — a rigid-sided box in our house obsidian, lined with tissue and ribbon. No extra charge for this; it's just how we ship. At checkout you can add a handwritten gift note. For special occasions, we also offer custom box embossing with a name or date for $12 — very popular for birthdays and "gotcha day" celebrations.
