Frequently Asked

We use only full-grain vegetable-tanned leather sourced from certified tanneries, solid brass and stainless steel hardware, and premium waxed canvas for our fabric pieces. Every material is chosen for longevity, beauty, and safety for your dog.
Each product page includes a detailed sizing guide. For collars and harnesses, measure around your dog's neck or chest with a soft tape measure and add 2 inches for comfort. When in doubt, size up — we offer free exchanges within 60 days.
We offer hassle-free 60-day returns on all items. If you or your dog isn't completely happy, reach out and we'll make it right. Our lifetime warranty also covers any manufacturing defects — no questions asked.
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the US. Express 2-day shipping is available at checkout. International orders typically arrive in 7–14 business days. Free shipping applies to all US orders over $75.

Wipe with a damp cloth after muddy adventures, let air dry away from direct heat, and condition with a quality leather balm every few months.
